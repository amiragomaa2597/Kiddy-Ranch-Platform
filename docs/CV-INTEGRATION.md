## Concise CV bullets (3–5)

- Built and maintained a nursery platform spanning a public marketing site, admissions workflows, enrolled student management, and financial transactions.
- Developed full-stack features with **Angular** (public SSR site + staff ops SPA) and **ASP.NET Core 8** REST APIs secured with **JWT** and Identity.
- Implemented student lifecycle tooling (profiles, registration requests, classes/buses) and finance modules (transactions, accounts, payment items, reports).
- Worked with **SQL Server / EF Core** models and migrations, including consolidation of student and invoice domains into a unified operations API.
- Delivered bilingual/Arabic-first staff UX and role/permission-aware access for multi-branch nursery operations.
